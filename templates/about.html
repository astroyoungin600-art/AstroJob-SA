About Astro Job SA - Made by Mthembisi Motakone

We help Mzansi find real jobs. Astro Job SA was founded in 2025 by Mthembisi Ineleng Motakone from Soweto/Orange Grove, Johannesburg. I am a Junior Cybersecurity Analyst (SOC L1, Penetration Tester) with validated Bugcrowd finding eToro P2 IDOR and founder of a live portal serving 684 users and 10,000+ views.

Why we exist: Youth unemployment in SA is high and job scams are everywhere. Our mission is to connect South Africans with legitimate jobs while protecting them from phishing and fake posts. Every job link on our platform is screened by our custom anti-phishing engine and SIEM dashboard that monitors 50+ security queries daily.

How we are different: Unlike pure aggregators, we add security layer - HTTPS verification, malicious link screening, POPIA compliance, and manual review of scam reports within 1 hour. Built with Flask 3.1.3, Python 3.11, secured on Render. Non-profit, community-driven, serving Gauteng, KZN, Western Cape, all provinces.
